Solvane: The Iron Reach

Star Trek ran almost permanently in the house I grew up in, and for most of my childhood I wanted to be an astronaut. Building the galaxy myself turned out to be the version of that I could reach. Started in 2016: an infinite procedurally generated galaxy you mine, build factories in, defend, and fly through in a ship you designed. Now on Steam, aiming at 2027.

About

I'm Anthony. I live in Las Vegas and I work at Zoox on the operations side of driverless cars, which puts me close enough to the interesting problems to want to be working on them.

Before code I drew, sculpted with clay and wrote stories, so making things was never the new part. Code just looked like math from the outside, tedious and unrewarding, until the first project came to life and turned out to be the same impulse pointed somewhere else. Now the part I care about is the layer under the thing you can see. How do you find the nearest thirty fish out of four thousand without checking all four thousand? How does a car plan a route through a road network the player is allowed to bulldoze while it's driving?

I'm not planning on a degree. I've watched too many people take on debt for a piece of paper and land in a job that would have hired them anyway. What I do instead is pick a problem I don't understand and stay on it until I do, which is how everything on this page got built.

I'm looking for entry-level or junior work in simulation, gameplay, graphics or tools, remote or here in Las Vegas. Get in touch if that's the sort of thing your team does.
