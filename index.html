Procedural generation · WebGL

Procedural Galaxy Generator

Type a seed like 7F8A-2C91-BD44 and you get a galaxy. Type it on someone else's machine and you get the same galaxy, down to the mountain ranges on a particular planet, because none of it is stored anywhere. It gets recomputed from those twelve characters every time. You can fly from a quarter of a million stars down to the ground.

  • three.js
  • Custom GLSL
  • Web Worker
  • sfc32 PRNG
  • Headless test suite

Agent simulation · Graph algorithms

Arterial — City Traffic Simulator

Draw some roads, paint some zoning, and a city grows along them. Then it jams. Every car picks its own route and follows the one ahead using a traffic model borrowed from the research literature, so the congestion figure in the corner is measured rather than invented. Bad junction placement shows up as a queue about a minute later.

  • Canvas 2D
  • IDM + MOBIL
  • A* routing
  • Fixed 60 Hz step

Flocking · Cellular automata

Aquarium & Cellular Automata Sandbox

Four thousand fish, and a bed of sand you can dig into while they're swimming over it. The fish were the easy part. Getting from three hundred of them to four thousand without the frame rate falling over meant throwing away the obvious data structures. There's a readout in the corner showing how much work the grid saves each frame.

  • Canvas 2D
  • Spatial hash
  • Typed-array pools
  • Zero dependencies

About

I'm Anthony. I live in Las Vegas and I work at Zoox on the operations side of driverless cars, which is close enough to the interesting problems to be frustrating. So I write simulations in my own time.

The part I care about is the layer under the thing you can see. How do you find the nearest thirty fish out of four thousand without checking all four thousand? How do you build a road network a car can actually plan a route through, when the player is allowed to bulldoze the road the car is driving on? Questions like that are why none of these projects sit on top of an engine. If I'd built the galaxy in Unity, what I would have learned is Unity.

I'm looking for work in simulation, gameplay, graphics or tools. If that's what your team does, say hello.
